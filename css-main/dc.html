BATMAN

Batman is a fictional superhero created by DC Comics. Unlike many heroes, he has no superpowers; instead, Bruce Wayne uses intelligence, martial-arts training, detective skills, and advanced technology to protect Gotham City. Driven by the loss of his parents, he becomes Batman to fight crime while operating from the shadows as a symbol of fear and justice.

SUPERMAN

Superman is a DC Comics superhero known for his extraordinary strength, speed, flight, and ability to protect people with compassion. Born on the planet Krypton as Kal-El, he is raised on Earth as Clark Kent and uses his powers to defend humanity. His hopeful spirit and strong moral values make him a lasting symbol of courage and responsibility.

AQUAMAN

Aquaman is a DC Comics superhero and the ruler of Atlantis, a powerful underwater kingdom. Also known as Arthur Curry, he can breathe underwater, communicate with sea life, and possesses great strength and endurance. As a bridge between the surface world and the ocean, Aquaman protects both realms from danger.
